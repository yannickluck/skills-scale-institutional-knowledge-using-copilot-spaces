# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme runs cross-functional projects using standardized, repeatable processes that accelerate delivery, encourage iterative improvement, and ensure stakeholder alignment. Our project management approach is designed to support teams across all functional areas, from initial concept through post-deployment retrospectives.

## Project Management Lifecycle

Our comprehensive project management framework encompasses the following key phases:

### 1. **Initiation**  
Define project scope, objectives, and stakeholder alignment to ensure all parties understand the project's purpose and expected outcomes.

### 2. **Planning**  
Develop detailed project plans including timelines, resource allocation, deliverables, and success metrics. This phase establishes the roadmap for execution.

### 3. **Execution & Tracking**  
Execute planned activities while continuously monitoring progress against objectives. Track metrics, manage dependencies, and keep stakeholders informed of status.

### 4. **Risk Management & Communication**  
Proactively identify, assess, and mitigate risks. Maintain consistent, transparent communication with all stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

### 5. **Release & Deployment**  
Prepare, coordinate, and execute the release of project deliverables. Ensure proper testing, documentation, and knowledge transfer before go-live.

### 6. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**  
Conduct post-project reviews to capture lessons learned, celebrate successes, and identify opportunities for process improvement on future projects.

### 7. **Roles & Personas**  
Clearly define project roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority. Understanding team composition and stakeholder personas ensures effective collaboration.
